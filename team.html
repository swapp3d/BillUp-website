Our Team

BillUp is built by a small, focused team of designers and engineers who believe that paying household bills should never be stressful. We combine product thinking, careful testing and friendly support to keep the experience simple.

Whether you are a resident tracking monthly expenses or a company collecting payments, the people below are working every day to make BillUp clearer, faster and safer.

  • Started 2026
